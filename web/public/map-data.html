Overture Maps

Overture Maps supplies roads, addresses, buildings, and retained land-use evidence. Transportation and buildings use ODbL 1.0. Addresses have provider-specific licenses. Streetlight normalizes and splits roads, infers some road names, estimates homes, assigns addresses, and selects building footprints.

The recorded geographic release identifies the applicable upstream notices, including OpenStreetMap, TomTom, Esri Community Maps contributors, Microsoft, Google Open Buildings, USGS, Qian Shi et al., IGN, and address suppliers:

USA Structures · ORNL/FEMA

Some U.S. maps include USA Structures, credited to Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) and the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Geospatial Response Office, under CC BY 4.0.

The distributed source includes Esri's documented field changes. Streetlight further filters and selects single-family footprints to fill confirmed gaps in Overture buildings. Stored polygons and source identifiers remain available with the adapted geography.

Free geographic data

Streetlight offers the corresponding adapted geographic database or complete geographic alterations, free over the internet in machine-readable form under ODbL 1.0. Source contents keep their notices and licenses.

Email bentheurich@gmail.com with the map's location and release or date. Include a packet code if you have one. No Streetlight account or church membership is required. Reuse is governed by the supplied licenses and does not require Streetlight's permission.

What the data includes

The offer covers normalized roads, names, estimates, assigned geographic addresses, and selected building footprints, including retained earlier geography. Church account information, packet assignments, and outreach or reconciliation history are excluded.

The public importer and normalizer records the transformation method. Copies use the stored geographic generation, including accepted FEMA footprints.
