Overture Maps

Overture Maps supplies roads, addresses, buildings, and retained land-use evidence. Transportation and buildings use ODbL 1.0. Addresses have provider-specific licenses. Streetlight normalizes and splits roads, infers some road names, estimates homes, assigns addresses, and selects building footprints.
