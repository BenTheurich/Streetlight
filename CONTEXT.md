# Streetlight language

These terms describe the existing outreach workflow. [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) defines its rules.

## Geography and assignments

**Street segment**:
A section of street tracked as one outreach unit, including homes on both sides. Its geographic
identity survives a later import when the source still identifies the same segment.

**Imported street version**:
The geography and estimated homes recorded for a street segment in one church's region at an
import. Older versions remain part of the history of packets prepared from them.

**Reservation**:
An active packet's claim on a street segment. Importing a newer version of the same segment does
not create another assignable street.

**Packet preparation**:
The administrator's workflow for reviewing proposals, finalizing selected assignments, and
downloading their printed sheets. Retrying a batch download refers to that same finalized batch.

**Outreach Progress**:
The reflective view of recorded outreach over a selected period, including its unattended
presentation and static print view. It is separate from the operational coverage heatmap.
